## About myself  
I am a normal guy, I am a swell guy, I am a nice enough guy in his early to mid twenties. You can refer to me as **pivnoy_chel** or **pivnya**. Those are my nicknames that I made up in 2018, that have stuck. **Pivnya** is an *affectionate diminutive* of **pivnoy_chel**.

## Music taste  
I have listened to music all my life. My preferences were constantly changing, from EDM to Blackened Death Metal. Thought, I have always had a knack for hard-rock and metal since the early childhood.  
  
Right now I mostly listen to Atmpospheric Black Metal, Shoegaze and Post-punk. I also really love atmospheric electronic music and ambient, like **Aphex Twin** and **Brian Eno**. I don't mind listening to mainstream pop music, but after listening such songs for too long I get a headache. I also hate most of the rap music.

## Vidya  
{{% image src="lain-pc.jpeg" alt="lain on pc" width="220" style="object-fit: cover; float:right;"%}}

Most of my life was spent playing videogames. This medium is takes a lot of time, and can only be compared to books in time spent. I found them magical as a kid, and was always drawn to them from early childhood.  
  
My first console was PS1. I think it was around that time that I developed a love for the **horror** genre, after playing such games as *Echo Night* and *Silent Hill*. Or after reading a ton of creepypastas and other spooky stories. I think after all the Echo Night was first. Although, at that time I preferred the *Duke Nukem Time to Kill* and *Quake 2*, as I was too afraid of horrors.  
  
Currently I am playing a lot less videogames, but I still have an attraction to them.

## Movies  
I am not an avid movie watcher. I have a significant backlog of movies that I am very slowly clearing out. To be honest, I never paid enough attention to this medium. Usually I marathon movies in 12 hours sessions once in 6 months.  
  
My idol is **Takeshi Kitano**. His works are something special for me.

Although I've begun to notice that the love for the genre in this medium is waning... I still prefer horror in games!
