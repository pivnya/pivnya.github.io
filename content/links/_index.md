{{% details summary="Religion and folklore" %}}
* [Theoi Project](https://www.theoi.com/)  
A Greek mythology encyclopedia featuring art and mentions in classical literature.  
* [The Encyclopedia Mythica](https://pantheon.org/)  
An online encyclopedia on mythology, folklore, and legends.  
* [ISTA 'The Internet Sacred Text Archive'](https://www.sacred-texts.com/)  
An archive of online books about religion, mythology, folklore, and occult topics.  
* [Yōkai](https://yokai.com/)  
An illustrated encyclopedia of yokai, Japanese mythical creatures.  
* [Blagovestnik](http://www.blagovestnik.org/)  
A Russian collection of audio, text and video Orthodox Bible materials.

{{% details summary="History" %}}
* [Old Maps Online](https://www.oldmapsonline.org/)  
A collection of historical maps with an interactive timeline.  
* [Wappenwiki](https://wappenwiki.org/index.php/Main_Page)  
A collection of coats of arms of countries in medieval Europe.  
* [Chadnet](https://wiki.chadnet.org/)  
A collection of translations, archived articles and other information on various topics including history, religion and philosophy.

* [The Internet Classics Archive](https://classics.mit.edu/)  
A collection of classical literature translated into English.  
* [Miles Mathis](https://milesmathis.com/)

* [Dither it!](https://ditherit.com/)  
A web tool for dithering images.  
* [Sora's Pixel Convertor](https://pixel-converter.ameniwa.com/)  
Much like the previous one, but converts images to pixel art and allows adding other effects, mainly for fun.  
* [Braille ASCII Art](https://lachlanarthur.github.io/Braille-ASCII-Art/)

* [femboy.beauty](https://femboy.beauty/)
